# Important Network ports in Win Environment
- 53: `DNS`.
- 88: `Kerberos`.
- 135: `WMI`/`RPC`.
- 137-139 & 445: `SMB`.
- 389 & 636: `LDAP`.
- 3389: `RDP`
- 5985 & 5986: `PowerShell Remoting` (`WinRM`)
